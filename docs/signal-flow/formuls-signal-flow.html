formuls signal flow

Audio network of src/faust/formuls.dsp (compiled to the Pd external formuls~) and how six instances are wired in src/pd/. Derived from formuls.dsp, fsynth.lib, ffx.lib, _main.pd, f.formuls~.pd and f.audio.*modroute~.pd.

Summary

  • One instance is a complete voice bank: a 20-voice round-robin FM oscillator bank (carrier plus an internal sine modulator, per-voice ADSR), then a 9-stage effects chain, envelope follower, volume, pan and mute. That is the whole of process(mod, env).
  • Two audio inputs: mod frequency-modulates the oscillators; env drives the envelope follower / sidechain that scales the instance output.
  • Six instances are not a fixed serial chain. Each wrapper (f.formuls~) contains two routing matrices (f.audio.modroute~) that let every instance pick which of the six instances (or none) feeds its mod input and which feeds its env input. The connect chain 1→2→…→6 in AUDIO_TRIPES is the control-message pass-through, not audio.
  • Master bus (per AGENTS.md, only reverb and repeater are compiled): instance outputs go to a reverb send, the rolling freezer/repeater, and the DAC.
  • Modulation is multiplicative on frequency: the external input scales the instantaneous frequency by (1 + input × extmodulation), and the internal modulator adds a relative sine term.

4. Output pair mapping, freezer and master bus (traced)

Inside f.formuls~ (canvas AUDIO_ROUTINGS) the external's four signal outputs plus the smoothed output-amp signal are recombined. The external's leftmost outlet is a message outlet and is unconnected.

So the "m" signals are mute-independent taps: muting an instance silences it in the mix but it still modulates other instances and can still be frozen or repeated. The env input of every instance is also multiplied by 10 in the wrapper before it reaches formuls~.

Rolling freezer internals (f.audio.rollingfreezer~, one per channel)

  1. Band-limit: hip~ 50 then lop~ 14000.
  2. Capture: the audio goes through f.audio.spectralpitchshift~ (the transpose control, semitones × 100) and is written continuously into a 192000-sample table (about 4 s at 48 kHz) and a 4000 ms delwrite~. A delayed read of that delay line is fed back into the write, controlled by feedback.
  3. Freeze: WRITE_TIMER stops table writes while freeze=1, so the last ~4 s of audio is held. On unfreeze there is a 4 s guard (pipe 4000) before the level can rise, so stale audio is not heard.
  4. Resynthesis: ROLLING_FREEZER_FFT_ uses block~ 65536 4 1 (overlap 4), a Hann window, rfft~ and rifft~, with noise~ passed through cos~ into the inverse-FFT inputs. That structure is consistent with phase-randomised spectral hold (Paulstretch-style); I inferred that from the objects rather than from a comment.
  5. Level: level is squared (pow 2) and slewed; below 0.01 the DSP is switched off (switch~) to save CPU.
